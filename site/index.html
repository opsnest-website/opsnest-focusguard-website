Screen-time limits that hold

FocusGuard locks an app when you've used up the time you gave it for the day. Turning protection off takes your password, and if you break a limit, someone you trust can find out.

What it does

Daily limits

Set minutes per app, with a different limit for weekends if you like. When time is up you get one minute to finish, then the app locks until tomorrow.

App groups

Give several apps one shared budget, for example 45 minutes of "Social" across all of them.

Blocked hours

Schedules that block apps at set times, such as bedtime or work hours, whatever time is left.

Accountability

Choose contacts who get a text or a call when you break a limit, so your limits don't depend on willpower alone. The full version sends the text automatically and can post a WhatsApp status.

Hard to bypass

A password, emergency codes, a delay before limits can be loosened, and protection against uninstalling, which is strongest in the full version.

Streaks and history

Daily history, a weekly report, streaks and badges for days you stay within your limits.

Private by design

Stays on your phone

Usage data, contacts and settings are kept on your device and encrypted. There are no FocusGuard servers.

Encrypted backups

Back up and restore your setup with a file protected by a passphrase you choose.

No tracking

No accounts, no analytics and no ad SDKs. Read the privacy policy.

Switching between versions

  • From Google Play to the full version: install the full APK over it. Your settings are kept, and Google Play stops offering updates for it.
  • From the full version to Google Play: make an encrypted backup in Settings, uninstall FocusGuard, install it from Google Play, then restore the backup.
  • Both versions are signed by OpsNest with the same key, and only one can be installed at a time.

Install the full version

  1. Open the releases page on your phone and download the newest .apk.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Open FocusGuard, create a password, and follow the setup steps to grant the permissions it needs.

Each release is signed with the same key, so newer versions install over older ones and keep your settings.
