FocusGuard Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how the FocusGuard Android app (package app.opsnest.focusguard), published by OpsNest, handles your data. FocusGuard helps you keep to app-time limits you set yourself.

Summary

  • FocusGuard has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Everything the app records stays on your device. We don't run servers that receive your data.
  • Data leaves your device only in the situations listed under When data leaves your device, and only because you turned those features on or started them yourself.

FocusGuard picks contacts through Android's contact picker. It does not read your address book.

When data leaves your device

  1. Accountability alerts (SMS and phone call). If you add accountability contacts and turn on alerts, FocusGuard sends an SMS from your phone, or places a call, to those contacts when a limit is broken or someone tries to remove or disable FocusGuard. The message says which app or limit was involved. Your mobile carrier delivers it, and normal carrier charges may apply.
  2. WhatsApp status (optional). If you turn this on, FocusGuard opens WhatsApp and posts the alert message as a text status. The people your WhatsApp status privacy settings allow can see it, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.
  3. Password manager (optional). If you choose to save your FocusGuard password, it is passed to your device's password manager (for example Google Password Manager) through Android's Credential Manager. The password manager's own privacy policy then applies.
  4. Update check (optional). If you turn on update checks, the app asks GitHub's public API whether a newer version exists. Like any web request, GitHub sees your IP address. No personal data is sent. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  5. Things you share yourself. Backups, emergency codes and debug reports go only where you send them, using Android's share menu. Backup files are encrypted with a passphrase you choose.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for any purpose other than the app's features.

Device administrator and device owner

If you turn on device administrator or device owner mode, FocusGuard uses it only to stop the app from being uninstalled or disabled without your password. It does not use these permissions to access your data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete everything by clearing FocusGuard's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app, after turning off protection with your password. SMS messages already sent to your contacts are kept on their phones, and we cannot delete them.

Children

FocusGuard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Sensitive values are encrypted with keys held in the Android Keystore. Because nothing is sent to us, there is no server copy of your data that could be breached.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
